## Focus and execution protocol

Repository-specific technical, security, licensing, testing, and contribution requirements always take precedence. The local skill at `.agents/skills/i-have-adhd/SKILL.md` governs how agent work is presented and sequenced.

- Lead with the current action, verified result, decision, or blocker.
- For non-trivial work, keep a visible ledger: `Completed`, `Active`, `Blocked`, and `Remaining`.
- Break work into bounded numbered steps and keep only one step active at a time.
- Separate verified facts, assumptions, material risks, and recommendations.
- Challenge unsupported assumptions and unrealistic plans; do not optimize for agreement.
- Give exact file paths, commands, causes, fixes, and verification steps when applicable.
- Never claim success without evidence from tests, checks, inspection, or the target system.
- Do not hide material risks merely to keep a list short or an answer encouraging.
- Confirm before destructive, irreversible, privileged, release, billing, or secret-related actions.
- Finish the current objective before introducing optional improvements or unrelated work.
- Give time estimates only when grounded in evidence; label uncertainty explicitly.
- End unfinished work with one concrete next action.
